# Annual Audit Preparation Calendar

TechSync is audited each year against SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001. This calendar shows the milestones for the compliance team and the DSEC engineers who supply evidence, so that audit season is a series of small steps rather than a scramble.

## How the year is organized

| Period | Focus |
|---|---|
| January to February | Close out last year's findings; refresh the control mapping |
| March | Kick off the SOC 2 observation review; assign evidence owners |
| April to May | Evidence collection and gap checks |
| June | Auditor fieldwork for SOC 2 Type II |
| July | Respond to auditor questions; remediation of any gaps |
| August | SOC 2 report issued; lessons-learned session |
| September | ISO 27001 surveillance audit preparation |
| October | ISO 27001 surveillance audit |
| November | Remediation of ISO findings |
| December | Planning for next year; control owner review |

Dates move by a few weeks each year depending on the auditors' schedule. The compliance team posts the exact dates in the compliance channel in January.

## What engineers are asked to do

1. **Confirm control ownership.** Each control in Compass has an owning team. Owners check their list in March.
2. **Provide evidence when asked.** The Compliance Evidence Handbook explains the format and how to upload to Compass.
3. **Respond to gap tickets within five business days.** The weekly gap check opens tickets when evidence is missing.
4. **Be available during fieldwork.** Auditors may ask an engineer to walk through how a control works. The compliance analyst will brief you beforehand.

## What the compliance team does

- Maintains the control mapping and the evidence tracker.
- Runs the weekly gap check during evidence collection.
- Coordinates all communication with the auditors.
- Reports status to the CISO.

## Tips for evidence owners

- Collect evidence as work happens, not at the end.
- Name files so someone else can find them.
- If you are unsure whether something counts, ask the analyst before the deadline.

## Contacts

The compliance channel, or the analyst assigned to your team on the evidence plan.
