# Access Review Policy v1

**Owner:** IT & Compliance | **Applies to:** all TechSync employees and contractors

## 1. Policy statement

Access to TechSync production systems is reviewed **twice a year, in January and July**.

## 2. Cycle

- Review cycles open on the first business day of January and July.
- Each reviewer has 20 business days to complete their review.
- IT & Compliance generates review lists from Keystone group exports.

## 3. Responsibilities

System owners confirm that each account is still needed. Managers confirm access for their direct reports. IT & Compliance chases overdue reviews and reports completion to the CISO.

## 4. Revocation

Access marked as no longer needed is removed within 5 business days.

## 5. Evidence

Completed review records are stored in Compass for the audit team.
