# Product Overview: TechSync Mobile

## In one sentence

TechSync Mobile puts the two things you most often need away from your desk, alerts and deploy approvals, on your phone.

## Features

### Alerts
Receive notifications when something needs your attention, whether it comes from a pipeline, a billing event or a monitoring condition. Each alert shows what happened, how severe it is, and what to do next. You can acknowledge an alert from the notification itself.

### Deploy approvals
When a release is waiting on a person, the right reviewers get a request on their phone. They can read the summary, see what is changing, and approve or reject without opening a laptop. Every decision is recorded in the same audit trail used by the web console.

### Preferences
Choose which alerts you want, set quiet hours, and decide how loudly each severity should interrupt you.

## Platforms

TechSync Mobile is available for iPhone and for Android phones. Details of supported operating system versions are maintained in the app's release notes.

## Who uses it

On-call engineers, release managers, and approvers who are not always at a keyboard. The app is included with every customer tier.

## How it connects to other products

- Alerts from **Insight** can be delivered to your phone.
- Approval requests from **Ship** arrive in the app.
- Notifications are delivered by **Pushline**, TechSync's notification service.
