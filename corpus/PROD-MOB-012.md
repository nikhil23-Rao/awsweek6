# Internal Beta Distribution Program

## Purpose

Before a release reaches customers, employees can try it. This catches problems that automated tests do not.

## Who can join

Any employee. Product Engineering teams are encouraged to join, and Support and Sales are welcome.

## How to join

1. Request access through the internal beta form.
2. Install the beta tracks: TestFlight for iOS, an internal testing track for Android.
3. Join #mobile-beta-feedback.

## What we ask of testers

- Use the beta build for your normal workflow.
- Report problems in the feedback channel with the version number and steps to reproduce.
- Try the main flows: viewing alerts, acknowledging them, approving a release.

## How builds are released to the beta

A new beta build is produced after every merge to the release branch. Testers get a notification when it is ready.

## Feedback loop

The team reads feedback daily. Anything reproducible becomes a Jira ticket, and testers are told when it is fixed.

## Leaving the program

Remove the beta track from your device or ask in the channel.
