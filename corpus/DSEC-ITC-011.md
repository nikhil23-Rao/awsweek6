# IT Helpdesk Intake FAQ

**How do I contact the IT helpdesk?**
Use the IT helpdesk page in Confluence, or post in the IT help channel. For anything that blocks your work, use the page so that a ticket is created.

**What can the helpdesk do?**
Laptop and peripheral issues, software installs, group membership requests, account problems, and onboarding setup.

**What can the helpdesk not do?**
Security exceptions, policy questions, and questions about specific systems owned by other teams. They route those to the right place.

**How do I get access to a system?**
Use the Keystone request portal and search for the group named in your team's onboarding guide. The group owner approves.

**My account is locked. What now?**
Contact the helpdesk. They confirm your identity and unlock it.

**I lost my laptop.**
Report it immediately through the helpdesk. They will disable your device and arrange a replacement.

**How long do requests take?**
Most are handled within one business day. Anything involving hardware purchases takes longer.

**Who approves software purchases?**
Your engineering manager approves, and IT & Compliance reviews the vendor.

**Can I install my own tools?**
Common developer tools are allowed. For others, ask the helpdesk; they will check with AppSec if there is a risk.

**How do I join a Slack channel I cannot see?**
Ask a channel member for an invite. The helpdesk does not manage channel membership.

**Who do I ask if my question is not here?**
The IT help channel.
