# Product Overview: TechSync Console

## One front door

TechSync Console is the shared web console for TechSync products. Rather than learning a different interface for every product, customers sign in once and find Ship pipelines, Ledger usage and billing views, and Insight dashboards under the same roof.

## What you will find in Console

- **A unified navigation.** Switch between products without losing your place.
- **Dashboards.** Build and share views that combine information from more than one product.
- **Account and team management.** Invite colleagues, manage roles and review activity.
- **Notifications.** See alerts and approval requests that need your attention.
- **Search.** A global search box that finds pipelines, services, invoices and saved queries.

## Design language

Console is built on **Prism**, our design system. Prism supplies the components, color tokens and accessibility patterns used across TechSync web interfaces, which is why different products feel consistent even though different teams build them.

## Who it is for

Everyone who uses a TechSync product uses Console, from an engineer checking a deploy to a finance analyst downloading a usage report. It is available to customers on the Free, Team and Enterprise tiers.

## Browser support

Console works in current versions of the major desktop browsers. Detailed browser guidance is kept by the team that builds Console, and customers can find it in the help center.
