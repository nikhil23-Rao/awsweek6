# DSEC New Hire Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Data & Security! We are glad you are here. This guide covers your first 30 days across the whole org. Your own team will add a team-specific guide on top of this one, and your manager will walk you through it.

A note before the details: nobody expects you to absorb everything in a week. Most people tell us the first month feels like drinking from a hose. That is normal, and we have tried to put the most important things first.

## Before day one

Your manager and IT will arrange your laptop and accounts. You do not need to do anything, but if something looks missing on day one, tell your buddy.

## Week 1: get settled

- Meet your manager and your onboarding buddy, who is an engineer on your team.
- Complete the company onboarding modules in the learning portal.
- Join the DSEC Slack channels: the main DSEC channel, your team's channel, and the DSEC docs channel.
- Request the group memberships your team lists through the Keystone request portal. Most requests are approved within a business day.
- Clone the main repositories for your team from GitHub Enterprise and build one of them locally.
- Read the DSEC Org Charter and Team Map and the Architecture Principles.

## Week 2: learn the systems

- Walk through the architecture of the systems your team owns with your buddy.
- Read the five most recent postmortems for your team's systems. They tell you more about real behavior than any diagram.
- Shadow an engineer on a routine task, such as a deploy or a ticket triage.
- Attend your team's planning, design review, and demo.

## Weeks 3 and 4: make a first contribution

- Pick a small ticket from the good-first-issue list on your team's board.
- Open a pull request and get it reviewed.
- Ship it. A small change in production in your first month is a goal we care about.
- Present what you learned at your team's demo.

## Who is who

| Team | Owns | Ask them about |
|---|---|---|
| Data Platform | Reservoir, Streamline | Data lake, event streams |
| ML Infrastructure | Foundry, FeatureHub | Training and serving |
| AppSec | Sentinel, Vaultline | Scanning, secrets |
| IT & Compliance | Keystone, Compass | Identity, audit evidence |

## Tools you will use

Jira for work tracking, Confluence for documents, Slack for conversation, GitHub Enterprise for code, PagerDuty for paging (through Lookout), and Terraform for infrastructure.

## Working remotely

TechSync is remote-first. Write things down, prefer async updates, and use video for the conversations that need it. Please put your working hours in your Slack profile.

## Your 30-day check-in

At the end of the month your manager will ask three questions: what is going well, what is confusing, and what would you change about this guide. Your answers go straight into the next edit.

## Help

Your buddy, your manager, and the DSEC channel. No question is too small.
