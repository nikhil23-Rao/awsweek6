# Technical Documentation Standard

Good documentation saves hours. This standard applies to documents kept in the shared Confluence spaces and repositories.

## Every document needs

1. A title that says what the document is for.
2. An owner team and the author's role.
3. A status: draft, approved, deprecated, or unmarked for working notes.
4. A last-updated date.
5. A short opening paragraph that says who the document is for.

## Templates

Technical Writing provides templates for runbooks, design documents, postmortems, onboarding guides, FAQs and policies. Start from the template; delete sections you do not need.

## Writing style

- Prefer short sentences and plain words.
- Put the most important instruction first.
- Use numbered steps for procedures and tables for reference values.
- Define abbreviations on first use.

## Review

Documents that other teams will rely on are reviewed by a peer before they are marked approved. Technical Writing is happy to review any document on request.

## Upkeep

Owners review their approved documents at least once a year and update the last-updated date when they do.
