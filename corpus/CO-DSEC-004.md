# Vulnerability Remediation SLA Policy v1

**Owner:** AppSec

## 1. Policy

| Severity | Fix within |
|---|---|
| Critical | 14 days |
| High | 45 days |

Medium and Low findings are handled in team backlogs.

## 2. Clock

The clock starts when the finding is confirmed.

## 3. Extensions

Extensions need written AppSec approval.
