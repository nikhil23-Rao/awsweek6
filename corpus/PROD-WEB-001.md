# WEB Team Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the Web team. We own Console and the Prism design system. This guide covers your first week and the things new engineers ask most often.

## Before you start

Your manager will have requested your accounts. By day one you should be able to sign in to email, Slack, GitHub Enterprise, Jira and Confluence. If anything is missing, tell your buddy, who will chase it.

## Week one

- **Day 1:** Meet the team. Join #prod-web and #prod-web-standup.  
- **Day 2:** Get Console running locally using the Local Development Setup page.  
- **Day 3:** Read the Prism Component Contribution Guide and look through the component library.  
- **Day 4:** Pair with your buddy on a small ticket.  
- **Day 5:** Open your first pull request and have it reviewed by two teammates.

## How the team works

We plan in two-week sprints, hold standup at 9:30 Pacific on Tuesdays through Fridays, and do planning on Mondays. Pull requests follow the company code review policy before merging, and we prefer many small changes to a few large ones.

## Setting up compute for a new team

If you are standing up a new team or service on Kestrel, plan for capacity early. When a new team is created on Kestrel, its namespace gets **60 CPU cores** by default. Raise a ticket with the platform team if you expect to need more, and include the workload you have in mind.

## Who to ask

- Day-to-day questions: your buddy.  
- Technical direction: Tech Lead.  
- People and growth: Engineering Manager.

## Tips from people who joined recently

Ask questions in public channels. Keep a list of terms you do not know. Say when you are stuck; no one minds.
