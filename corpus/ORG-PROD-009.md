# Product Engineering Week-One Onboarding Checklist

Welcome. Your manager and your onboarding buddy will help you through this list. Do not worry about finishing everything in five days.

## Day 1

- [ ] Laptop set up and company accounts working (email, Slack, GitHub Enterprise, Jira, Confluence).
- [ ] Meet your manager and your buddy.
- [ ] Join your team channel and the #prod-announcements channel.

## Days 2 and 3

- [ ] Read the Engineering Handbook and the product overview for your team's product.
- [ ] Clone your team's main repository and get it building locally.
- [ ] Attend your team's standup and planning meeting.

## Days 4 and 5

- [ ] Pick up a starter ticket your buddy has chosen with you.
- [ ] Open your first pull request. Small is good.
- [ ] Have a first one-on-one with your Tech Lead.

## Access to more systems

Some access, such as access to production systems, comes later and is requested through your manager. Ask your buddy how that works for your team.

## By the end of week one

You should have shipped a small change, met your teammates and know where to ask for help. Write down what confused you and share it with your manager. It helps us improve this list.
