# Access Review Policy

**Owner:** IT & Compliance | **Applies to:** all TechSync employees, contractors, and systems

## 1. Policy statement

TechSync reviews who has access to its systems **every quarter**. This policy takes effect in February 2024; the first quarterly cycle opens in April 2024.

## 2. Scope

All systems that hold production access, customer-derived data, or administrative privileges are in scope. This includes services of every tier: Tier 0 core platform, Tier 1 customer-facing, Tier 2 internal, and **Tier 3 experimental**. Being a Tier 3 service does not exempt it from review.

## 3. Cycle

- Review cycles open on the first business day of January, April, July, and October.
- Each reviewer has 15 business days to complete their review.
- IT & Compliance generates the review lists from Keystone group and entitlement exports.

## 4. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| System owner | Confirms every account and entitlement is still needed |
| Reviewing manager | Confirms access for their direct reports |
| IT & Compliance | Opens cycles, chases overdue reviews, reports completion |
| CISO | Receives the completion report each cycle |

## 5. Revocation

Access marked as no longer needed must be removed within 2 business days of the decision.

## 6. Evidence

**Evidence of every completed review must be uploaded to Compass**, for every in-scope system, Tier 3 included. Evidence consists of the exported access list, the reviewer's decisions, and the ticket showing removals. Reviews with no evidence in Compass are treated as not completed.

## 7. Escalation

Reviews not finished by the end of the window are escalated to the reviewer's engineering manager, and then to the CISO, Tamsin Reid.

## 8. Audit

The completed-review records in Compass are provided to our SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 auditors on request.
