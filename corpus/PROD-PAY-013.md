# Meterflow Usage Export FAQ

*For customers and Support.*

**What is a usage export?**
A file that lists the usage Meterflow recorded for your account over a period you choose.

**How do I request one?**
In Console, open **Usage > Exports**, choose a date range and format, and select **Create export**. You will get an email when it is ready.

**Which formats are available?**
CSV and JSON.

**How far back can I export?**
As far back as your account has data, up to the limit in your plan.

**How long does an export take?**
Most finish in a few minutes. Large ranges can take longer.

**Why do my numbers differ from my invoice?**
Exports show raw recorded usage. Invoices apply pricing, minimums and credits. Compare the usage totals first, then check the pricing plan.

**Can I schedule exports?**
Yes. In **Usage > Exports**, choose **Schedule** and select daily, weekly or monthly.

**Will late events change an export?**
Recent windows can change slightly as late events arrive. The export marks windows as final or not final.

**Who can I ask?**
Contact support, or the Payments team if you are an internal user.
