# Access Review Onboarding Guide for Compliance Analysts

Welcome to IT & Compliance. Access reviews are one of the biggest recurring pieces of work for analysts, and this guide walks you through what a cycle looks like so you are not surprised when your first one starts.

## The calendar

TechSync runs access reviews **twice a year**. Cycles open on the first business day of **January** and **July**. Reviewers get 20 business days to finish. The rest of the year is for audit preparation, SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 evidence work, and cleaning up the previous cycle's loose ends.

Put the next two cycle start dates in your calendar on day one.

## Your first cycle, step by step

1. **Week -1:** Request the Keystone group and entitlement exports for the systems assigned to you. Check that the owner listed for each system is still correct.
2. **Day 1:** Create one review ticket per system and assign the system owner and the managers.
3. **Day 1:** Send the kickoff message in the access-review Slack channel with the due date.
4. **Weeks 1-3:** Answer reviewer questions. Typical ones: "Who is this contractor?" and "Why does this group have admin?" Your answer is usually in Keystone or the HR system.
5. **Day 15:** Send reminders to anyone with open tickets.
6. **Day 20:** Escalate unfinished reviews to the reviewer's manager.
7. **After close:** Confirm that every "remove" decision has a matching removal ticket, then store the exports, decisions, and removal tickets in Compass.

## Tips from the team

- Keep a running list of reviewers who are repeatedly late; managers appreciate a heads-up before the window opens.
- Contractors are the most common source of surprise accounts.
- Do not edit exports. If a number looks wrong, ask the Keystone team to regenerate.

## Who to ask

Your onboarding buddy, the ITC Tech Lead, or the compliance channel.
