# ISO 27001 Surveillance Audit Notes

Notes from the debrief after last cycle's ISO 27001 surveillance audit. Written for the next analyst who owns the audit, so they can see what was asked and what to prepare. Informal.

**Date of debrief:** 6 Nov 2025 | **Present:** two analysts, the ITC engineering manager, and a representative of the CISO's office

## Overall

The auditors completed the surveillance audit with two minor observations and no major nonconformities. They described TechSync's documentation as easy to follow, which was a nice change from the first year.

## What they asked about

- **Asset inventory:** how we keep the Compass inventory of systems current. We showed them the regular refresh and the owner list.
- **Supplier management:** how vendors are reviewed. They sampled three and asked for the review records.
- **Incident handling:** they picked two recent postmortems and asked how actions were tracked to completion.
- **Secure development:** they asked how scanning is built into pipelines and how findings are tracked.
- **Training:** records that engineers completed required courses.

## Observations

1. **Supplier reviews:** one review had no recorded date. We have added a date field to the template.
2. **Document control:** one policy page had no visible owner. The owner was fixed during the audit.

Both are minor; each has a ticket and an owner.

## What worked

- Having a named person per control meant answers were quick.
- The evidence index was complete, so we did not hunt for files.
- Engineers who were interviewed had been briefed and were relaxed.

## What to do differently

- Start the supplier record check earlier.
- Do a document-ownership sweep a month before the audit.
- Offer auditors a shorter walkthrough of Compass at the start; they spent time finding their way around.

## Actions

| Action | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Add date field to supplier review template | Analyst | done |
| Run ownership sweep on policy pages | Analyst | before next audit |
| Prepare a 15-minute Compass tour | Lead analyst | before next audit |

## Notes for next time

The auditors were friendly and fair. They said they prefer to be told early about anything that changed in the year rather than discover it. A short "what changed" list prepared in advance would help.
