# DSEC Interview Rubric

A short guide for interviewers on the DSEC hiring loop and how to score candidates.

## The loop

1. Recruiter screen
2. Hiring manager conversation
3. Technical interview: coding or practical exercise
4. Systems design interview
5. Collaboration and values interview
6. Debrief

## Scoring

Each interviewer scores one competency from 1 to 4:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 4 | Strong evidence; clearly above the bar |
| 3 | Meets the bar |
| 2 | Some concerns; mixed evidence |
| 1 | Clear concerns |

Write your notes before you read anyone else's. Scores and notes are submitted before the debrief.

## Competencies

- **Technical depth** in the relevant area (data, ML, security, or identity)
- **Systems thinking:** failure modes, trade-offs, operations
- **Communication:** clarity, listening, written clarity
- **Collaboration:** how they work with other teams
- **Ownership:** follow-through and judgment

## Debrief rules

- Start with the lowest scores.
- Talk about evidence, not impressions.
- The hiring manager decides after listening to everyone.

## Don'ts

Do not ask about family plans, age, or health. Do not compare a candidate to a former colleague.
