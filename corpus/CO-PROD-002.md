# Product Overview: TechSync Ship

**Launched:** 2016
**Category:** CI/CD and release orchestration

## What Ship does

TechSync Ship takes code from a pull request to production. It builds artifacts, runs the checks a team has configured, and orchestrates the rollout across environments (dev, staging and prod). Release managers and engineers use Ship to see, in one place, what is deployed where and what is waiting to go out.

## Core capabilities

- **Build and test orchestration.** Pipelines are defined as code alongside the service they build.
- **Environment promotion.** A release moves from dev to staging to prod with explicit gates between each step.
- **Release orchestration.** Coordinate several services shipping together, with ordering and dependency awareness.
- **Audit trail.** Every promotion, approval and rollback is recorded and searchable.
- **Approvals.** Reviewers can approve a promotion from the web console or from TechSync Mobile.

## Who uses it

Ship is used by platform and application teams at customers of every tier. Internally, TechSync's own engineering teams use Ship for their services, so the product gets daily exercise from the people who build it.

## How Ship fits with other products

- **Console** is where most people interact with Ship day to day.
- **Mobile** carries deploy approval requests to the people who need to act on them.
- **Insight** can receive deploy events so that a release marker appears on a graph next to the metrics it may have affected.

## Getting help

Customers can reach support through their normal support channel. Internal engineers can find the Ship team through the Product Engineering directory. For roadmap questions, talk to your account team.
