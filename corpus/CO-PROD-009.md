# TechSync Customer Service Level Agreement

**Owner:** CTO office
**Effective:** January 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document states the availability commitment TechSync makes to customers for its hosted products. It applies to every customer, and the commitment depends on the customer's tier.

## 2. Availability commitment

| Customer tier | Monthly availability commitment |
|---|---|
| Enterprise | 99.95% |
| Team | 99.9% |
| Free | 99.9% |

The Enterprise commitment of 99.95% took effect in January 2026. The Team and Free commitments remain at 99.9%.

## 3. How availability is measured

Availability is measured per calendar month. It is the percentage of minutes in the month during which the hosted service was able to respond successfully to requests, measured from TechSync's monitoring at the edge of our network.

As a rough guide to scale, a 99.9% month allows about 43 minutes of unavailability, and a 99.95% month allows about 21 minutes.

## 4. What does not count as downtime

- Scheduled maintenance announced to customers in advance.
- Unavailability caused by the customer's own configuration, network or systems.
- Events outside TechSync's reasonable control, such as widespread internet outages.

## 5. Reporting and credits

Customers who believe the commitment was missed should open a support request within the period stated in their order form. TechSync will review the monitoring data and, where the commitment was missed, apply the remedy described in the order form.

## 6. Status information

Current and historical status is published on the TechSync status page. Incident updates follow the customer-facing communication template used by Product Engineering.

## 7. Questions

Questions about this document go to the CTO office. Questions about a specific contract go to your account team.
