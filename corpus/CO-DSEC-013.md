# Information Security Policy Framework Overview

This page explains how TechSync's information security policies fit together and where to find each one. It deliberately contains no requirements; each policy states its own.

## How the policy set is organized

The framework has three layers:

1. **Company-wide policies** apply to every employee and contractor. The CISO's office sponsors them, and each has a named owning team.
2. **Org-wide standards** describe how an org applies company policies in its own work.
3. **Team runbooks and guides** explain how a team operates its own systems.

When you need an answer, start at the most specific layer that applies to you, then check the layers above it for anything your team must follow.

## Who owns what

| Policy area | Owning function |
|---|---|
| Access reviews | IT & Compliance |
| Production access | IT & Compliance |
| Vulnerability remediation | AppSec |
| Secrets rotation | AppSec |
| Open-source licenses | Legal, through AppSec |
| Data classification | Office of the CISO |
| Log, trace and dataset retention | Data Platform |

## Where documents live

Policies are published in Confluence under the Security space. Approved versions carry an owner and a last-reviewed date. Older versions are archived and marked so that audit history is complete.

## How policies change

1. The owning team drafts the change and opens a review ticket.
2. Stakeholders from affected teams comment for two weeks.
3. The CISO approves.
4. The Technical Writing group publishes the new version and announces it in Slack.

## Audit and assurance

TechSync maintains SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications, and IT & Compliance maps each policy to the relevant controls in Compass.

## Getting help

Ask in the compliance channel, or contact the owning team listed above.
