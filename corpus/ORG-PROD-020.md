# Working with the SRE Guild and TPM: FAQ

**What is the SRE guild?**
A group of SREs from across the three engineering orgs. They share reliability practice and look after policies that apply to every team. Each product team has SREs who work with it day to day.

**When should I involve an SRE?**
When you are designing something that must stay up, when you want help setting alerts, when you are preparing for a large launch, or when an incident needs more hands.

**What is a TPM?**
A Technical Program Manager. TPMs coordinate work that crosses team boundaries, keep planning calendars and run dependency reviews.

**When should I involve a TPM?**
When your work depends on another team, when a deadline slips and others are affected, or when you are not sure which team owns something.

**How do I reach them?**
Post in #prod-tpm or #sre-guild-help. For urgent production problems, page the on-call engineer instead.

**Can the SRE guild or TPMs approve my change?**
They advise and help you find the right people. Approvals follow the policy that applies to your change.

**Where do I learn more?**
The Engineering Handbook describes cross-org functions, and the Product Engineering Team Directory lists contacts.
