# Q2 2026 Delivery Retrospective

*Written by the TPM function from team retrospectives. This is a blameless review of how the quarter went.*

## Summary

Product Engineering delivered most of its committed work in Q2. Web shipped the dashboard performance improvements and the first set of Prism updates. Mobile released the alert-preferences changes. Payments finished the usage export work. Search delivered improvements to the query planner.

## What went well

- **Early dependency reviews.** Teams that listed dependencies in the planning meeting rarely got stuck.
- **Smaller releases.** Teams that released weekly found problems sooner and rolled back less.
- **Documentation.** Runbooks written during the quarter were used in two real incidents.

## What did not go well

- **Late scope changes.** Three items grew mid-quarter without a matching reduction elsewhere.
- **Test environment contention.** Staging was busy near the end of the quarter, and teams waited for their turn.
- **Handoffs.** A few tickets bounced between teams before landing with the right owner.

## What we will do differently

1. Add a one-page scope-change note whenever planned work grows.
2. Look at staging capacity before the next end-of-quarter crunch.
3. Use the Team Directory when routing tickets.

## Thanks

To everyone who covered for colleagues on leave and wrote the clear handoff notes.
