# Platform Interview Loop Guide

**Owner:** Platform engineering managers

## Loop structure

| Stage | Length | Interviewer | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruiter screen | 30 min | Recruiting | Background, interest, logistics |
| Technical screen | 60 min | Platform engineer | Coding in Go or Python |
| Onsite: systems design | 60 min | Staff engineer or tech lead | Designing infrastructure at scale |
| Onsite: operations | 60 min | SRE | Debugging a production scenario |
| Onsite: coding | 60 min | Platform engineer | Practical coding |
| Onsite: hiring manager | 45 min | Engineering manager | Collaboration, ownership, growth |

## What we look for

- **Systems thinking:** understands how components fail and interact.
- **Operational judgment:** stays calm, gathers data, communicates during incidents.
- **Code quality:** readable, tested, appropriately simple.
- **Ownership:** cares about the outcome for users, not just the task.

## Rubrics

Each interviewer scores on a 1 to 4 scale for each focus area and writes evidence for every score. Scores without evidence don't count.

## Debrief

Held within two business days. The hiring manager decides; any "strong no" must be discussed.

## Interviewer expectations

- Complete interview training and shadow two interviews first.
- Submit feedback within 24 hours.
- Don't discuss the candidate with other interviewers before submitting feedback.

## Candidate experience

Tell candidates the format in advance. Leave five minutes for their questions in every session.
