# Insight Access and Entitlements

**Team:** Search  **Audience:** Support, Sales engineers, Search engineers

## Overview

TechSync Insight is currently in beta. This guide explains who can use it and how access is granted.

## Who has access

**Insight is available to Enterprise customers only.** Free and Team customers cannot use Insight during the beta.

## Granting access

1. The customer's account team requests access using the beta access form.
2. The Search Engineering Manager approves the request.
3. Support enables the Insight entitlement on the account.
4. The customer's admin receives an email with a link to the Insight onboarding steps.

## Limits during the beta

- Up to 10 GB of ingested data per day per account.
- Up to 20 saved searches per user.
- Community support through the shared channel plus business-hours email support.

## Common questions

**A Team customer asks about Insight. What do we say?**
Insight is in beta for Enterprise customers. Take their details and share with the account team.

**Can a trial be arranged?**
Only for existing Enterprise customers evaluating Insight.

## Feedback

Send beta feedback to #insight-beta.

## Changes

Entitlements are reviewed monthly as the beta progresses.
