# Platform New Engineer Onboarding Guide

*Technical Writing, Platform Engineering*

Welcome to Platform Engineering. This is your guide to the first 30 days.

## Week 1: Get set up

- Finish the company-wide onboarding from People Operations.
- Set up your laptop with the *New Engineer Local Development Setup* guide.
- Read the Platform Engineering Org Charter and the Platform Service Catalog.
- Meet your onboarding buddy (your manager will introduce you).
- Join #platform, your team channel, #platform-releases, and #platform-incidents.

## Week 2: Learn the systems

- Read your team's charter and the top five runbooks for your team's systems.
- Deploy a sample service with Scaffold to dev and watch it through Gatekeeper CI.
- Find your sample service's traces in Tracewell and its dashboard in Lookout.
- Sit in on your team's weekly planning.

## Week 3: First change

- Pick a "good first ticket" from your team's Jira board.
- Get it reviewed, merged, and released.
- Attend a Platform incident review meeting.

## Week 4: Go deeper

- Read three recent Platform postmortems.
- Shadow your team's on-call for a day.
- Write or improve one runbook. Fresh eyes find gaps.

## 30-day check-in

Meet with your manager to talk about what's working, what's confusing, and when you'll join the on-call rotation.
