# Engineering Handbook: Orgs, Roles and Cross-Org Functions

*Part 2 of the Engineering Handbook. Part 1 covers working agreements.*

## The shape of engineering

Engineering is organized into three orgs, a structure in place since the 2022 reorganization. All three VPs report to the CTO, Arjun Mehta.

| Org | Code | VP | Teams |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform Engineering | PLAT | Dana Whitfield | Compute, Networking, Observability, Developer Tooling |
| Product Engineering | PROD | Carlos Mendoza | Web, Mobile, Payments, Search |
| Data & Security | DSEC | Rachel Goldstein | Data Platform, ML Infrastructure, AppSec, IT & Compliance |

Each team has an Engineering Manager and a Tech Lead. The directory for each org lists current names and the systems each team owns.

## Roles you will see in documents

- **Engineering Manager:** accountable for a team's people, delivery and health.
- **Tech Lead:** sets the technical direction for the team and reviews designs.
- **Staff Engineer:** owns technical problems that cross a team's boundary.
- **SRE:** works on reliability, on-call practice and incident response.
- **Security Engineer:** works on application and infrastructure security.
- **TPM:** coordinates programs that span teams or orgs.
- **Technical Writer:** maintains documentation standards and publishes company-level documents.
- **Compliance Analyst:** tracks evidence and obligations for audits and certifications.

Documents in the library cite authors by role rather than by name.

## Cross-org functions

Four functions sit across the three orgs:

1. **Technical Program Management** runs planning and coordination for multi-team work.
2. **The SRE guild** brings SREs together to share practice and owns shared reliability policy.
3. **Technical Writing** maintains standards and writes company-wide documents.
4. **The Architecture Review Board,** chaired by the CTO, reviews architectural decisions that matter beyond a single team.

## Company leadership

CEO Helen Park, CTO Arjun Mehta, CISO Tamsin Reid, CFO Lucia Romano and VP People Jerome Baptiste make up the leadership group referenced throughout the handbook.

## Finding the right team

Start from the directory for the org you think is closest. If you are unsure, ask in the engineering help channel and a TPM will point you.
