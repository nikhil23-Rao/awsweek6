# DSEC Quarterly Planning Process

Technical Program Management runs a planning cycle for DSEC each quarter. This page describes the calendar, the artifacts, and who does what, so that nobody is surprised by a deadline.

## Calendar

| Week | Milestone |
|---|---|
| -4 | TPM opens the planning doc and shares last quarter's results |
| -3 | Teams draft objectives and key results (OKRs) |
| -2 | Tech leads review cross-team dependencies |
| -1 | Engineering managers and the VP review the combined plan |
| 0 | Plans are published; the quarter starts |
| 6 | Mid-quarter check-in |
| 12 | Quarter closes and scores are recorded |

## Artifacts

- **Team OKR page:** 3 to 5 objectives per team, each with measurable key results.
- **Dependency table:** every request one team makes of another, with an owner and a date.
- **Capacity sheet:** engineers available after on-call, leave, and holidays.

## Roles

- **TPM:** runs the calendar, chases drafts, and publishes the final plan.
- **Engineering managers:** own their team's commitments.
- **Tech leads:** own the technical feasibility of each key result.
- **VP:** resolves trade-offs between teams.

## Rules of thumb

1. No more than 70 percent of a team's capacity is planned; the rest is reserved for incidents and unplanned work.
2. A key result that depends on another team must have that team's agreement written in the dependency table before the plan is published.
3. Changes after week 0 are logged in the planning doc, not edited silently.

## Scoring

Key results are scored from 0.0 to 1.0. A score of 0.7 is considered a good result for a stretch goal. Scores are discussed in the retrospective, not used in individual performance conversations.
