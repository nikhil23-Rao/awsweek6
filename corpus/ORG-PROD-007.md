# Product Engineering Quarterly Planning Process

Each quarter, the four Product teams agree on what they will deliver. This page describes the cycle so that planning is predictable.

## Timeline

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| -6 | TPM publishes the planning calendar and company priorities |
| -5 to -4 | Teams draft proposals with their Product partners |
| -3 | Cross-team dependency review run by the TPM |
| -2 | VP review of the combined plan |
| -1 | Teams finalize commitments and capacity |
| 0 | Quarter begins; plans are published |

## Inputs

- Company and org priorities
- Customer feedback and support trends
- Reliability and tech-debt backlogs
- Team capacity, after time off and on-call load

## Outputs

Each team publishes a one-page plan: goals, planned work, dependencies on other teams, and known risks. Plans are reviewed at the mid-quarter checkpoint.

## Principles

Commit to fewer things and finish them. Leave room for the unplanned. Name dependencies early and in writing.

## Contact

Questions go to the TPM for your team or to #prod-planning.
