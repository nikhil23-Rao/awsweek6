# SRCH Team Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Search. We own Quarry, the query engine, and Sieve, the indexer. These two systems power search inside Insight.

## First week

- **Day 1:** Meet your manager and buddy. Join #prod-search.
- **Day 2:** Set up your laptop and clone the Quarry and Sieve repositories. Read the Sieve Indexing Pipeline Overview.
- **Day 3:** Run the local stack, which starts a small OpenSearch node and sample data.
- **Day 4:** Read the Query Language Syntax Guide and try ten queries.
- **Day 5:** Fix a starter ticket with your buddy.

## How we work

We run two-week sprints. Planning is on Mondays, and demos are on the second Friday. We care about careful, measured performance changes: every change comes with a benchmark.

## Things to read

1. Quarry Query Planner Internals
2. Relevance Tuning Basics FAQ
3. OpenSearch Cluster Capacity Planning
4. Sieve Reindexing Runbook

## On-call

You will join the rota after two months. Before then, shadow a few shifts.

## People

Your Engineering Manager is your first stop for questions about role and growth; your Tech Lead for technical direction.

## Tips

Read other people's pull requests. Ask what the benchmark showed. Write down what you learn.
