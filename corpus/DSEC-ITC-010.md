# Compass Control Mapping Reference

Compass tracks controls and the evidence that shows they operate. This reference shows how our control families map to the frameworks TechSync is audited against, SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, and who owns the evidence.

## How to read the table

- **Family:** the group of controls in Compass.
- **SOC 2 area:** the broad trust services area.
- **ISO 27001 theme:** the broad Annex A theme.
- **Evidence owner:** the team that supplies evidence.

| Family | SOC 2 area | ISO 27001 theme | Evidence owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Change management | Common criteria: change management | Operational security | Developer Tooling, SRE guild |
| Incident response | Common criteria: incident handling | Incident management | SRE guild |
| Business continuity | Availability | Continuity | SRE guild |
| Vendor management | Common criteria: risk mitigation | Supplier relationships | IT & Compliance |
| People security | Common criteria: control environment | People controls | People team |
| Asset management | Common criteria: system operations | Asset management | IT & Compliance |
| Secure development | Common criteria: change management | Secure development | AppSec |
| Risk management | Common criteria: risk assessment | Risk assessment | CISO's office |

## Using the mapping

1. Find your system's family in Compass.
2. Open the control and read what evidence is expected.
3. Upload or link the evidence as described in the evidence handbook.

## Adding or changing a mapping

Mapping changes are made by IT & Compliance after review with the auditors. Raise a docs or change request in the intake queue.

## Notes

- A single control can satisfy more than one framework requirement.
- Auditors may request evidence outside the table; ask the compliance channel if you are unsure who owns it.
