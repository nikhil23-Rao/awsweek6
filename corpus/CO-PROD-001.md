# TechSync Company Overview

## Who we are

TechSync Software builds developer-platform products for businesses that ship software at scale. The company was founded in 2014 as TechSync Labs and is headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Since 2020 we have been remote-first: most of our roughly 2,400 employees work from home offices across many time zones, and about 900 of them are in engineering.

## A short history

TechSync Labs started with a small team and a single idea: release automation should not require a dedicated team to babysit it. That idea became **TechSync Ship**, which launched in 2016. In 2019 we moved our own infrastructure from virtual machines to Kubernetes, a move that shaped how we build everything since. Remote-first working began in 2020, and in 2021 we launched **TechSync Ledger** for usage metering and billing. In 2022 engineering was reorganized into three organizations, which is the structure we use today. In 2023 we completed SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 audits. **TechSync Insight**, our logs, metrics, traces and search product, followed in 2024.

## What we sell

- **TechSync Ship**: CI/CD and release orchestration.
- **TechSync Ledger**: usage metering and billing.
- **TechSync Insight**: logs, metrics, traces and search.
- **TechSync Console**: the shared web console that fronts our products.
- **TechSync Mobile**: alerts and deploy approvals on your phone.

Customers buy on one of three tiers: Free, Team and Enterprise. Each product has its own overview page in the company library.

## How we run

TechSync runs on AWS. Our primary region is us-east-1, disaster recovery lives in us-west-2, and customer data that must stay in the European Union is hosted in eu-west-1. Our day-to-day stack includes Kubernetes on EKS, Go, Python, TypeScript, Kotlin and Swift, PostgreSQL, Kafka, OpenSearch and Terraform. We collaborate through GitHub Enterprise, Jira, Confluence and Slack, and we use PagerDuty for paging.
