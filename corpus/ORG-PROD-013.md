# Engineer Interview Loop Guide

This guide explains how Product Engineering interviews engineers, so that candidates have a consistent experience.

## Stages

1. **Recruiter conversation** (30 minutes).
2. **Technical screen** (60 minutes): a coding exercise on a shared editor.
3. **Onsite loop** (four sessions, remote): coding, system design, a deep dive on past work, and a conversation about collaboration.
4. **Debrief:** interviewers meet to share written feedback.

## Interviewer responsibilities

- Read the candidate's materials beforehand.
- Use the question bank for your session; do not improvise questions that others cannot repeat.
- Submit written feedback within 24 hours, before reading anyone else's.
- Rate against the rubric, not against other candidates.

## Rubric areas

Problem solving, code quality, design thinking, communication, and working with others. Each area is scored from 1 to 4 with written examples.

## Fairness

Ask the same core questions of every candidate for a role. Do not ask about age, family, health or any protected characteristic. If a candidate raises one, move on politely.

## Contact

The recruiting partner for Product Engineering coordinates scheduling.
