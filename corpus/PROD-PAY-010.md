# PAY Working Agreement

How the Payments team works together. Everyone on the team agreed to this, and we revisit it each quarter.

## Rhythm

- **Standup:** daily at 9:15 Pacific, fifteen minutes, in the team channel thread or on video.
- **Planning:** every second Monday.
- **Retrospective:** every second Friday afternoon.
- **Demos:** the last Friday of each month.

## Working in the open

We prefer public channels. Write down decisions in the ticket or pull request so people on leave can catch up.

## Handoffs

If you will be away, write a handoff note: what is in flight, what is risky and who to ask. Put it in the team's handoff page.

## Pull requests

Small pull requests are easier to review. Aim to respond to review requests within one working day.

## On-call

The rota is shared across the team. Swaps are fine; tell the channel. After a rough night, take the morning off without asking.

## Disagreement

Disagree openly and kindly. If we cannot settle it, the Tech Lead decides, and we move on.

## Looking after each other

Ask how people are. Cover for each other. Say thank you.
