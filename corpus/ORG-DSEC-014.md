# DSEC Learning and Reading Program

We try to keep learning visible in DSEC. This page lists the regular activities and how to join in.

## Book club

A new book every six weeks, chosen by vote. Recent picks were about distributed systems, writing, and incident response. The club meets for 45 minutes every other Thursday. You do not need to finish the book to come.

## Brown bags

Informal 30-minute talks over lunch on the first Wednesday of the month. Anyone can present: a design you are proud of, an incident you learned from, a tool you like. Sign up on the brown-bag page in Confluence. Recordings are posted for people in other time zones.

## Paper reading group

Every second Tuesday, a small group reads one paper (often from systems, ML, or security conferences) and discusses it. The presenter picks the paper at least a week ahead.

## Conference and course learning

Engineers can attend one external conference or course each year, subject to the learning budget process described by your manager. After attending, please share notes at a brown bag or in the docs channel.

## Mentoring

DSEC runs a mentoring match twice a year. Mentors and mentees are matched by interest, not by team. The sign-up form is posted in the DSEC channel.

## Shadowing

If you want to see how another DSEC team works, ask your manager to arrange a one-week shadowing. People often return with ideas for their own team.

## Organizing

The program is run by a rotating group of three volunteers. If you want to help, post in the learning channel.
