# Platform Engineering Org Charter

**Org:** Platform Engineering (PLAT)
**VP:** Dana Whitfield, reporting to CTO Arjun Mehta

## Mission

Platform Engineering builds the foundation every TechSync product runs on: compute, networking, observability, and the developer workflow. We succeed when product teams ship quickly and safely without thinking about infrastructure.

## Principles

1. **Paved roads.** Make the right way the easy way.
2. **Self-service.** Teams shouldn't need to file a ticket for routine work.
3. **Own it in production.** We operate what we build.
4. **Write it down.** Decisions go in ADRs; procedures go in runbooks.

## Relationship to other orgs

- **Product Engineering** and **Data & Security** are our main customers.
- **AppSec** sets security requirements that Platform systems must meet.
- **The SRE guild** spans all orgs; Platform SREs are active members.
- **The Architecture Review Board** reviews cross-team changes.

## Planning

Platform plans in half-year cycles with quarterly reviews. The roadmap is published in Confluence.

## History

Platform Engineering formed in 2022 when engineering reorganized into three orgs.
